Privacy Policy

Global News Intelligence

Effective date: November 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Global News Intelligence uses information when its administrator connects a YouTube channel to the publishing system.

2. Information We Access

With the account owner's authorization, the application may access YouTube account and channel information necessary to identify the selected channel and upload videos on the owner's behalf.

3. Use of Google and YouTube Data

YouTube data and permissions are used only to provide the authorized publishing functionality, including uploading videos to the connected YouTube channel.

The application does not use YouTube data for advertising or sell such data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and publishing information are handled by the publishing system configured by the channel administrator. Appropriate measures should be taken to protect credentials and prevent unauthorized access.

5. Data Sharing

Personal information and YouTube data are not sold. Data is not shared with third parties except where necessary to provide the requested service or comply with applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The channel administrator can disconnect the YouTube account and revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.

Stored credentials and publishing records should be deleted from the publishing system when they are no longer required.

7. Third-Party Services

The application uses Google and YouTube services. Their handling of information is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy .

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the administrator of Global News Intelligence through the associated project contact email.
